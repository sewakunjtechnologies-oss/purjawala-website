HOW TO OPEN THE WEBSITE
-----------------------
Double-click:

    purjawala-website.html

That single file IS the whole website. Everything is inside it — all five
catalogs, the PDF reader, the 3D hero and the product images.

  · No folders to arrange, no installation, no server
  · Works with the internet switched off
  · Opens in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, on laptop and on phone
  · First open takes a few seconds (it is a large file); after that it is instant

The only thing that needs internet is the Google map in the Contact section.
Offline, it shows the address card instead — nothing looks broken.

TWO THINGS WORTH DOING, NEITHER URGENT
--------------------------------------
  1. Product names.
     Of the 553 catalogued parts we could illustrate, only 211 carry a
     name — the rest have a code and a photograph only. Nobody searches
     Google for "V-211", but they do search for "vamatex plus 9000 bevel
     gear". Someone looking at the photographs can name about 30 an hour.
     A week of odd moments would clear the lot, and it is the single
     highest-return task available. It needs no developer.

  2. Photograph your top 100 fastest-moving parts.
     The catalog images are roughly 170 pixels wide — fine as thumbnails,
     soft when shown large. A phone and a sheet of white paper is enough.

  Also, for the 2027 print run: the catalog masthead reads "Importers &
  Traders". You are manufacturers as well, and the line understates you.
  "Manufacturers, Importers & Traders" is stronger, and it is true.
  ("Suttleless" on every interior page should also read "Shuttleless".)
